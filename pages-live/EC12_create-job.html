      I will onboard them myself

      Onboarding is verifying and gathering a subcontractor's details before they start — ABN, insurances, licences, White Card, superannuation, tax and bank details.

      What e-contract does: we assist with digital document preparation and transmission only. We don't collect, verify or store onboarding documents, and we don't provide legal advice. (Digital onboarding — a standard Commencement Pack — is coming soon.)

      What you're responsible for (including but not limited to): verifying the subcontractor's ABN, insurances, licences, White Card and bank details yourself, and checking your own obligations for workers compensation, long service leave and superannuation (these may apply depending on the engagement and your state). You remain responsible for verifying all information about this subcontractor.
